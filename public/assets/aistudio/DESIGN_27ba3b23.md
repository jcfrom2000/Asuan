## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful warmth, editorial authority, and psychological safety. Tailored for an integrative psychotherapy practice specializing in cross-cultural identity, couples, sex therapy, and life transitions, the atmosphere avoids clinical sterile coolness. Instead, it feels like an organic, tranquil sanctuary—reminiscent of tactile handmade linen paper, morning sunlight over water, and quiet botanical studies.

The design philosophy unites:
- **Minimalist Editorial Elegance:** Generous negative space, intentional typographic pacing, centered poetic pull quotes, and delicate structural rules.
- **Organic Earthiness:** Warm linen canvas, grounding moss and pine greens, warm sand undertones, and tactile stone pill buttons.
- **Bilingual Grace:** Thoughtfully integrated language switches (English / 繁體中文 / 简体中文) designed as balanced sculptural elements rather than utility afterthoughts.
- **Gentle Human Geometry:** Asymmetrical organic image framing (offset pastel backdrops, pure circle vignettes, soft chamfers) that softens traditional clinical layouts.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen light with profound, grounding darks to evoke emotional restoration and presence.

- **Primary (`#2E382B`):** Deep Forest Moss. Used for primary CTAs, deep focal backgrounds, prominent quote banners, and anchor headings.
- **Secondary (`#C8B6A6`):** Warm Sand Stone. Used for subtle card fills, photo backdrop offset blocks, badge backings, and refined divider borders.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6855`):** Muted Olive Sage. Used for secondary interaction states, botanical accents, subtle icons, and meta tags.
- **Neutral Core (`#1C1E1B`):** Deep Carbon Charcoal. Delivers high-contrast, effortless legibility for body copy and navigation without the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas System:**
  - Base Background: `#F7F4EE` (Warm Woven Linen)
  - Surface Alt / Cards: `#EFE9DF` (Soft Oat Canvas)
  - Deep Section Fill: `#2E382B` (Evening Moss) with `#F7F4EE` text
  - Border Hairlines: `#DDD5C7` (Sand Grout)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical literary warmth with crisp humanistic legibility:

- **Headlines & Quotes (Playfair Display):** Light, delicate serifs with poetic grace. Display treatments use relaxed letter-spacing and rhythmic line-heights to slow the reader's breath. Pull quotes and scripture/client reflection excerpts feature italicized or normal-weight styling flanked by atmospheric vertical spacing.
- **Body & Functional Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, highly legible modern sans-serif with subtle geometric warmth. Body text maintains a tall 1.75 line-height ratio for frictionless, accessible reading across varying emotional states.
- **Bilingual Harmony:** Chinese glyphs (e.g., 中文 toggle) render seamlessly alongside English headers with matched visual weight and baseline optical centering.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout uses a generous, breathing fluid grid structured around mindful pacing and unhurried editorial composition:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop (`margin-desktop: 4rem`, max container width of 1240px); collapses to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile (`margin: 1.25rem`).
- **Vertical Pacing:** Distinct sections are separated by large rhythmic margins (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`). Every content cluster is bordered by quiet contemplation zones to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Image Offsets:** Portraits and botanical vignettes feature intentional visual counterweights—such as a circular crop floating over an asymmetric sand rectangle or an image overlapping a divider line.
- **Horizontal Affirmation Ribbon:** A dedicated infinite horizontal ticker running between core narrative segments with floral ornaments and mindful affirmations ("You are enough ✿ You are welcome here").

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a natural, organic environment, this design system avoids heavy shadows, artificial glows, and slick glossy plastic finishes.

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is conveyed by physical layering of flat earth substrates (e.g., `#F7F4EE` canvas layered with `#EFE9DF` panels and `#C8B6A6` graphic backing blocks).
- **Subtle Surface Definition:** Cards and floating modules rely on low-contrast hairline borders (`1px solid #DDD5C7`) rather than drop shadows.
- **Deep Immersion:** Strategic contrast transitions into monolithic full-bleed dark moss (`#2E382B`) sections with serene white typography for deep reflection quotes and final call-to-actions.
- **Focus & States:** Interactive cards shift background tone subtly by 3% warm luminance upon hover rather than lifting dramatically on the z-axis.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular blends gentle, architectural softenings with circular harmony:

- **Base Corner Radius:** Standard inputs, service cards, and image containers utilize soft `4px` to `8px` (`roundedness: 1`) radius, evoking cut linen cardstock and framed art plates.
- **Pills for Action:** Interactive button elements, primary CTAs, and language pills use full circular radiuses (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to invite safe, tactile touch.
- **Circular Cameos:** Organic round portrait masks (aspect-ratio 1:1, border-radius 50%) juxtapose rigid rectangular layouts, symbolizing wholeness and self-reflection.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Dark Moss):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#1C1E1B` or `#2E382B` fill, `#F7F4EE` label. Subtle scale and opacity transition (`opacity: 0.9`) on hover. Compact vertical padding (`0.65rem 1.75rem`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** Clean sand outline (`1px solid #DDD5C7`), transparent background, charcoal text. Fills with `#EFE9DF` on hover.
- **Bilingual Switcher:** Fixed or header-anchored pill button (`#1C1E1B` background, crisp light serif/sans glyphs) allowing instant English/Chinese switching with refined tactile clarity.

### Service & Consultation Cards
- **Editorial Service Tile:** Crisp rectangular container with `#EFE9DF` background, padded evenly (`space-md`), holding a 4:3 or 1:1 muted botanical or abstract photo up top. Serif title, two-to-three line empathetic description, and a centered micro-pill CTA ("Learn More").
- **Consultation Feature Box:** Split layout pairing an intimate botanic macro image alongside serif appointment availability copy ("15min free consultation").

### Affirmation Ticker (Marquee)
- Continuous fluid horizontal bar with subtle borders above and below. Playfair serif font with center-aligned botanical glyphs (✿) separating serene grounding statements.

### Pull Quotes & Narrative Cards
- Wide, centered typographic sanctuaries. Large quotation marks or clean horizontal framing rules. Paired with attribution lines in 12px uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with 0.1em letter spacing.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat linen background with a `1px solid #DDD5C7` bottom border or subtle full hairline border, 8px radius, deep carbon input text, and warm olive placeholder tones.
