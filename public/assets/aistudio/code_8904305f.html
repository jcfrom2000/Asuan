The Therapist Behind OverStream

Aoxuan (Jessica) Cao

Psychotherapist • Cross-Cultural Specialist • Couples & Sex Counselor

Welcome! I am a licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW in IL, LMSW in NY & MI) and Clinical Professional Counselor (LCPC). Holding a master's degree in Social Work from the University of Chicago and a master's degree in Health Communication from Indiana University, I bring an interdisciplinary, trauma-conscious, and culturally humble framework to our therapeutic alliance.

As a first-generation immigrant and former international student, I intimately understand the quiet ache of isolation, intercultural code-switching, filial obligations, and the visceral survival anxiety that accompanies building a home between worlds.

I specialize in walking with 1st, 1.5th, and 2nd generation immigrants, international scholars, tech and creative professionals, and interracial/intercultural couples ready to redefine belonging on their own terms.
